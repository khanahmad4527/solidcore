#### WR-675. A curve sweep along a closed path caps both ends, leaving two coincident caps where the end meets the start
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp`, `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateCurveSweep`, which with `bCapEnds` always makes a planar start cap from the first side faces' start iso curves and an end cap from the last side faces' end iso curves, whatever the path. `CreateSweepAlongPlanarPath` in the same file skips its caps on a closed path.
- **What happens:** when the path closes on itself and the profile comes back where it started (a planar circle with the follow frame), the two caps lie on each other. After `StitchAndOrient` the body has two bounded regions instead of one closed tube. A boolean with it goes wrong: cutting it from the torus it lies inside gives an empty body, or status 1001 from `relate` with the other opt-in fixes on.
- **Trigger:** an 8 mm square in the XZ plane swept with `SMLIB_SWEEP_FOLLOW` and `bCapEnds` along an arc of radius 30 about Z from 0 to 360 degrees (tangency-labs/solidcore#2192; SolidCraft #2005). 180 and 359 degree sweeps of it are single closed solids.
- **How found:** SolidCraft regression case `fixtures/solidcraft/2005-closed-sweep-cuts-torus.json` (tangency-labs/solidcore#2191).
- **Confirmed:** no. The Rust port, which follows `CreateCurveSweep`'s capping, gives two regions; the C++ was not run.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With the opt-in `Kernel::fixes.curve_sweep_closed_path_joins_ends` (own design, not in the fork), `PrimitiveCreation::curve_sweep` leaves both caps off when every side face spans the whole sweep and its section at the sweep's end lies on its section at the start within the B-rep's tolerance (`sweep_ends_coincide`, 17 samples across each face). `StitchAndOrient` then joins the end edges to the start edges: the square's tube has 5 faces, 10 edges and 5 vertices, one region and 64 * 2 pi * 30 within 0.003 mm^3, and its cut from the torus around it is exact to 0.004 mm^3. A path that does not close, or a profile that comes back turned or scaled, is capped as before. Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/issue_2192_closed_curve_sweep.rs`.
