#### WR-645. Rounding a drafted pocket's rim and corner edges leaves a corner patch's edge off the corner round (0.845 at R3, 10 degrees)
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Blends
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp:2982`, `SmFilletCorner::MakeVariableRadiusFillet` (def. `:2492`), called from `MakeAnalyticCornerSurface` (`:3625`) for a four-sided corner with one rail-rail interpolation edge: the new variable-radius fillet's rail on the opposite fillet replaces the opposite edge's curve (`pOppositeEdge->SetCurve(pRail1Curve)`), with no check that the rail stays on that fillet's surface (only the rail-rail curve is tested, `TestRailRailInterpolationCurve`).
- **What happens:** `SmApiFilletEdges` with radius R on the 4 rim edges and the 4 corner edges of a pocket whose walls are drafted (lean out from the floor) succeeds, but at three of the four top corners the end edge between the corner round and the corner patch lies off the corner round. At each top corner two convex rim rounds meet the concave corner round (a concave N x N corner of four edges: the corner round's end cross-section, two rim-round cross-sections and a rail-rail interpolation on the top face). `CalcCrossSection` gives the corner round's end edge its exact arc, but `MakeVariableRadiusFillet` then turns the corner into a variable-radius fillet between the top face and the corner round, and its rail on the corner round, which replaces that end edge, runs past the end of the round (its surface's domain) by up to 0.42 at R3, 10 degrees. The edge's tolerance and its two vertices' are raised to twice that (0.844781), and `SmBrep::ValidatePointers` fails test #59 (edge tolerance relative to the B-rep's) three times and test #82 (vertex tolerance) six times, so SMLib's own check refuses the result. The gap grows with the radius and the draft (R3: 0.173 at 2 degrees, 0.845 at 10, 1.561 at 20; R1 at 10 degrees: 0.282). With vertical walls the rail is the end edge and every corner is exact. At the fourth corner the conversion is refused earlier and the corner is the patch on its exact edges, valid; which corner that is depends on how the pocket was built.
- **Trigger:** an 80 x 80 x 40 block minus the pocket whose floor square (side `s = 60 - 30 tan 10 = 54.710191`) is at z = 25 and whose walls lean out 10 degrees to the top (built as the box z 25..45 intersected with four boxes each turned 10 degrees about a line through a floor edge, so the walls meet at corner edges), then `SmApiFilletEdges(brep, {4 rim edges at z = 40, 4 corner edges}, 3.0, 1, 1, 1.0)`. The bad edges run across the corners at z = 37.521, e.g. (13.392, 69.563) to (10.437, 66.608).
- **How found:** the model of tangency-labs/solidcore#968 (a SolidCraft regression case), while writing its regression test (`crates/solidcore/tests/issue_968_drafted_pocket_floor_cut.rs`, which does not check the rounded body for this reason).
- **Confirmed:** yes. A standalone SM_API driver linked against the pinned reference libraries (v1.0.1, `3fd5b95`, the prebuilt `bin/cpp`) gives 23 faces, 52 edges, volume 206417.544076, the same three edges with tolerance 0.844781 and the same 9 failed `ValidatePointers` reports. The Rust port gives the same body.
- **Rust port:** ported as is by default. Opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_corner_variable_radius_rail_on_opposite_fillet` (`UpstreamFixes`, default `false`): `FilletCorner::make_variable_radius_fillet_with` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_corner/p2.rs`) refuses the conversion, as it refuses a crossing rail-rail curve, when any of 9 points of the new rail on the opposite fillet drops onto that fillet's surface (its natural domain) more than ten times the corner's tolerance away; the corner is then the patch. With it every corner of the trigger is exact (largest edge tolerance 4e-5) and the check passes, at 2, 10 and 20 degrees and at R1 and R3, with the same faces, edges and vertices; with vertical walls the body is unchanged. Test: `crates/solidcore/tests/wr_645_drafted_pocket_corner_rounds.rs`. Every difftest runs with the field off.
